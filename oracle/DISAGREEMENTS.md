# Oracle vs engine disagreements (triage log)

Classification: `engine bug` / `oracle bug` / `legal ambiguity`. Cite the clause row id from `clauses.md` and its `file:line`.

One row per distinct disagreement where the engine is LAXER than the oracle, or where the two cannot be compared through their interfaces. Engine strictness (the engine requires more than the oracle) is fine under the comparison rule and is not logged here; it is counted in the summary test of `test/oracle-diff.test.ts` (clear space on road and canal edges, MR 33 art. 4 not reduced by the gap, 9,999 m2 low regime, 50 m2 per hotel room at rooms = floor(GFA / 50), ord. art. 50 small-dwelling exemption not applied, 2x on every road, relief only within 60 m of the wider road, no ord. art. 49 para. 2 relief).

`test/oracle-diff.test.ts` allowlists D1, D2, D3, D6 and D7 by id: a laxer case passes only if re-running the oracle with that row's engine reading substituted makes the two agree. Every other laxer case fails the test. Resolution is the owner's call; all rows are open.
